Czech airspace

Everything the published airspace carries, plus the airfields it leaves out: a zone around each of the 82 public aerodromes, and the traffic circuit at 156 fields and ultralight strips. A paraglider may fly inside the zone but must stay out of the circuit, and no instrument draws either. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, hover for the name and limits.

Czech local time. Aerodrome hours are published for 68 of the 82 aerodromes and for none of the 74 ultralight strips, and nothing else on this map carries hours at all — so this hides part of one layer, not the map. Outside published hours is not “closed”: almost every field adds “otherwise on request”, and a tow launch needs no published hour.
