Planner

Everything openAIP carries over Czechia, plus what no published source draws: the traffic circuit at 157 fields and ultralight strips. A paraglider may fly inside an aerodrome zone but must stay out of its circuit, and no instrument draws the circuit. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, hover for the name and limits. Each zone is the box it really is — floor to ceiling — so drag with the right button to tilt and see what sits over what. The vertical is exaggerated five times, which the × buttons under the map set: at true scale, across 500 km of country, a 300 m traffic circuit is a third of a pixel tall and every box here is two rings on top of each other. ×1 is one press away, and the limits in each label are the real ones whichever setting you are on. The 37 that run above 4 km are cut off at a dashed lid, which is a cap and not their ceiling; the label says how high they go. Plan a task on the same map: press Draw a task and click turnpoints, and the route is scored as XContest would and checked against every zone it crosses.

Czech local time. Aerodrome hours are published for 68 of the 82 aerodromes and for none of the 74 ultralight strips, and nothing else on this map carries hours at all — so this hides part of one layer, not the map. Outside published hours is not “closed”: almost every field adds “otherwise on request”, and a tow launch needs no published hour.

Press Draw a task, then click the map to drop turnpoints. Drag, pinch and twist still move the view — a click that moved is a drag, not a point.

Scored with the same rules as the flight report: every side at least 28% of the perimeter for FAI, a closing gap under 20% of it for a closed course, multipliers 1 / 1.2 / 1.4. With ČPP chosen, the Czech cup's: closing under 5%, and in the Central European zone 1 / 1.8 / 2.2 (rules). Airspace is drawn for Czechia only. This is a plan, not a clearance.
