LayersEverything openAIP carries over Czechia, plus what no published source draws: the traffic circuit at 157 fields and ultralight strips. A paraglider may fly inside an aerodrome zone but must stay out of its circuit, and no instrument draws the circuit. Hover a zone for its name and limits. Each zone is the box it really is — floor to ceiling — so drag with the right button to tilt and see what sits over what. The vertical is exaggerated five times, which the × buttons under the map set: at true scale, across 500 km of country, a 300 m traffic circuit is a third of a pixel tall and every box here is two rings on top of each other. ×1 is one press away, and the limits in each label are the real ones whichever setting you are on. The 37 that run above 4 km are cut off at a dashed lid, which is a cap and not their ceiling; the label says how high they go.

Aerodrome hours are published for 68 of the 82 aerodromes and for none of the 74 ultralight strips, and nothing else on this map carries hours at all — so this hides part of one layer, not the map. Outside published hours is not “closed”: almost every field adds “otherwise on request”, and a tow launch needs no published hour.
