Verdict
Flyable when the wind blows up a slope this takeoff works in. Wind only.
Lid
A stable layer below 4 km: lapse under 2 °C/km; “inv” where it warms with height.
Thermal top
Two estimates: ECMWF’s mixing height (dashed) and a dry parcel lifted from the 2 m air (ring). The parcel is usually lower.

Welcome to parainsights

Free tools for paraglider pilots, split into three steps: where to go, what to fly, and how it went.

  • Meteo You are here

    Is it worth driving anywhere today, and where?

    • Compare any takeoffs side by side, four days ahead
    • The airgram, with the thermal top and the cloudbase
    • The sounding, and flymet's meteogram for the nearest airfield

  • Planner

    What is that task worth, and which airspaces do I need to check?

    • Draw a task on a 3D map, see its XContest score and export it to XCTrack
    • Every airspace it crosses, floor to ceiling, plus Czech ATZs and circuits
    • The sun and the wind at your takeoff time
    Open the Planner

  • Flights

    How did that flight go?

    • Drop in your IGC or KMZ; it is analysed in your browser, nothing is uploaded
    • Every climb and glide, a 3D replay, compare with friends
    • A debrief of where the flight could be improved
    Open Flights

The tabs at the top of every page switch between the three.
