Traffic circuits (okruhy) — drawn by this tool, not a published boundary. Shape and side measured off each field's VOC chart (112 runways, to about 50 m; the rest a standard box on the side the text states), the mirror only where the AIP says gliders fly it; altitude published where stated, otherwise 1 000 ft above the field (measured mean 993 ft over five, spread 820–1 150). At an SLZ strip the runway itself is reconstructed from the reference point and a heading rounded to 10°, so those are marked estcircuit drawn from the VOC charts, altitudes and runway tables from the AIP VFR manual, thresholds from OurAirports—
